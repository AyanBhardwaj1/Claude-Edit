# Hooks

The first 1-3 seconds decide whether anyone watches the rest. Hooks come
from `library/hooks.md`: fill-in-the-blank templates with `[slots]`.

## Process
1. Know the video: read `work/transcript.txt` (or the script). Write one
   sentence on what the payoff is: the result, the reveal, the lesson.
2. Shortlist 3-5 templates from **different categories** that point at that payoff.
   Fill every slot with specifics from this video: real numbers, the real tool,
   the real outcome. Never leave a generic slot like "this tool".
3. Score each one, out loud and briefly:
   - **True**: does the video deliver it? (no clickbait the footage can't pay off)
   - **Specific**: number, name, or concrete outcome
   - **Short**: 12 words or less spoken, 6 words or less as on-screen text
   - **Fits the footage**: the opening shot or line supports it
4. Use the winner two ways:
   - On-screen: EDL `"title"` (6 words max, can differ from the spoken line).
   - Spoken: if a line in the transcript already matches a strong hook, move that
     segment to the front of `segments` (cold open). If none does and the user
     can re-record, give them the exact line to film.
5. Tell the user the pick, the runner-up, and why.

## Rules
- Specific beats clever: "This cut my edit time from 3 hours to 10 minutes" beats "You won't believe this".
- Don't repeat the hook verbatim as both speech and text; let the text add context.
- Match the creator's voice. Swap words in a template until it sounds like them.
- Series content ("Day 80 of ..."): keep the series tag in the caption/text and still lead with the payoff.
- When a reel performs, add its hook under "Your winners" in `library/hooks.md` with date and views.
