# Viral hook library

Fill-in-the-blank opening lines for short-form video. `[slots]` get replaced
with specifics from the video. A hook works when it is **specific** (numbers,
names, real outcomes), **true** (the video pays it off), and **fast** (spoken in
under 3 seconds, 12 words or less). Pick 3-5 candidates from different
categories, adapt each to the video, and choose the one that the first seconds
of footage can actually deliver.

Used by the `hooks` skill. Add your own winners at the bottom with their view counts.
